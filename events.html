Where to put a table

Today is 28 July 2026. Autumn is the region’s fair season and it is still ahead of you. The big spring festivals have already run, so those are 2027 applications — and the organisations that get booths are the ones that ask in the autumn, not the week before.

The autumn run — three fairs, twenty-two days

All three take vendor booths. One banner, one table kit, three parishes, one autumn.

Nothing else on this site comes close to that reach per dollar

These are also the only hard deadlines in the whole plan. Everything else can slip a week without cost. These cannot — when the booth list closes, it closes for a year.

What goes on the table

A table with a banner and nothing to hand over is a wasted Saturday. Six things, and only six.

01

The earned card

What the M-TOC does on site, what to bring (DD-214), the phone number, and the next three deployment dates. Back: the eight programmes in one line each. Print two thousand.

02

A sign-up sheet

Name, phone, parish, branch. On paper is fine — captured is what matters.

03

The intake QR code

Straight to the Get Help form, for the ones who will not stop to talk.

04

One banner

With the phone number readable at ten feet. Most people walk past; the number is the message.

05

A veteran on the table

Not a staffer. In a rural parish this is the whole difference — people talk to their own.

06

Nothing unverified

No pilot dashboard figures on anything printed. Not one flyer, not one handout.
