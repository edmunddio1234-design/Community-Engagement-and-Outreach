Eight parishes: where to put a table, who to call

Louisiana Region 9 — defined identically by the Department of Health, the Emergency Response Network and GOHSEP — is Livingston, St. Helena, St. Tammany, Tangipahoa and Washington. Add East Baton Rouge and the two Felicianas, which sit in Region 2, and you have all eight historic Florida Parishes.

Why the region line matters

State agencies fund, staff and convene by region.

Five of your eight parishes answer to Region 9; three answer to Region 2. Anything you take to the state — emergency planning, public-health outreach, aging services — runs through two different regional structures with two different sets of people. Knowing that before the first meeting is the difference between one call and four.

One piece of luck runs the other way: all eight parishes sit inside a single Area Agency on Aging. One relationship reaches every senior centre in the footprint.

The clerk of court play — the best idea in this plan

Louisiana clerks of court record a veteran’s honorable discharge papers free of charge, and once recorded those documents are confidential — releasable only to the veteran or someone they designate. That is state practice, not a favour.

Think about what that means. Every clerk of court office in these eight parishes is a room where veterans voluntarily identify themselves, holding the exact document your intake asks for. No outreach event reaches a better-qualified person. Nobody else is working this channel.

The ask is small: a card holder on the counter, and one sentence from the clerk — “there’s a group that helps with benefits, here’s their card.” No cost, no liability, no paperwork.

What this plan does not know

Stated plainly, so nobody prints a guess

Vendor fees for every event except the Washington Parish Fair · 2027 dates for the spring festivals · six of the eight clerk names · West Feliciana’s governing body and meeting schedule · St. Helena’s meeting day · the Tangipahoa and Feliciana LDVA itinerant schedules · the Catholic diocesan split · and any parish-level ministerial alliance.

All of it is a phone call away — and in parishes this size the call is the first contact, which is the point. Verify every line before it is printed, published, or put in front of a funder.
